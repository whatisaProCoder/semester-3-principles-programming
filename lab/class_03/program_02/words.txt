The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. The dog was not happy.
In fact, the dog barked, barked, and barked again! Why was the dog barking?
Well, the fox had taken the dog's toy, and the dog wanted it back.

Sometimes the fox runs fast, sometimes the fox runs slow. But the dog, oh the dog,
never gives up. "Come here, fox!" the dog shouted. The fox laughed, the fox ran.
The dog chased, the dog barked, the dog tripped—but still, the dog got up.

Finally, the fox stopped. The fox looked at the dog. The dog looked at the fox.
They both stared for a moment... and then the fox dropped the toy.
The dog wagged its tail. The fox grinned. Peace at last?
